Privacy policy

Paint a Picture · Last updated 16 November 2026 (session recordings include the map)

Paint a Picture is a goal journal set in a small pixel world. This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps, where it goes and what you can do about it. The short version: your goals and journal are yours, we never sell or share them, and you can delete everything at any time.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you make (your places, pieces, goals, milestones, journal days and notes) is saved on your device first.
  • Full-size photos you add stay on your device. Only a small preview of each is synced if you have an account.
  • If you use the app without an account, nothing you write leaves your device. It's lost if you delete the app.

If you create an account

  • You sign in with your email address and a one-time code. There is no password.
  • Your world (the same things listed above, with small photo previews) is stored with our database provider, Supabase, so it's kept safe and is the same on every device you sign in on. Your first name and email are saved with it.
  • Only you can read your world. It's tied to your account and protected so no other user can see it.

Usage counts and session recordings (only if you allow it)

  • During the introduction, the app asks whether it may collect anonymous usage data. You can say no and the app works the same.
  • If you say yes, we also make session recordings: a replay of taps, scrolls and how screens look, including your map and your character, so we can see where people get stuck. The app's own words show; everything you write or type (your name, email, pieces, goals, milestones, journal, notes) and your own photos are hidden on your phone before anything is sent. Your map can show the names of your places and pieces.
  • If you say yes, we count which screens and steps are used (for example, "finished the introduction"), using PostHog on its EU servers, with a random ID that isn't linked to your name or email.
  • We never send your name, email, goals, milestones, journal text or photos, in counts or recordings. Usage counts never include place names; recordings can show them on your map.
  • Nothing is counted in Demo Data mode.

What we can never see

These are hidden on your phone before anything is sent, so you can write freely:

  • Your name and email
  • Anything you type
  • Your pieces, goals and milestones
  • Your journal, notes and to-dos
  • Your photos

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No one reads your goals or journal.

Other services

  • On the website, fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google. The iPhone app carries its own fonts and doesn't.
  • The optional "Help test Paint a Picture" form is run by Tally. Anything you type there is covered by your choice to fill it in and by Tally's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and your saved world from our database.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Children

Paint a Picture isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. For questions about your data, use the feedback option in TestFlight or the Help test form.
